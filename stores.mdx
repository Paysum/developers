---
title: "Stores"
description: "Storefronts, subdomains, custom domains and team access."
---

<p>
	A store is your storefront, its catalogue and its settings. You can run several stores from one
	account.
</p>

## Storefront address

<p>
	Every store is served from its own subdomain, <code>your-store.paysum.io</code>. Checkout links
	created through the API point at that address.
</p>

### Custom domains

<p>
	Connect a domain you own from the <strong>Custom domain</strong> page of your dashboard. Once it is
	verified, customers can reach the storefront on your domain instead of the subdomain.
</p>

## Design

<p>
	Pick a theme from the gallery, then adjust it in the visual builder or, for full control, the code
	editor.
</p>

## Team access

<p>
	Invite teammates from the Team tab. Invitations are accepted by link and members only see the stores
	they were invited to.
</p>
