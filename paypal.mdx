## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the correct method">
    PayPal and PayPal Friends & Family are separate methods with different setup and verification requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the setup panel">
    Open the PayPal method in payment settings and follow the account and callback instructions shown there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review IPN instructions when applicable">
    If your method requires IPN, configure the exact notification URL shown by the dashboard. Do not substitute a storefront URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify with an order">
    Check the receiving account, the payment record, and the platform order status after completing the transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Callbacks and confirmation

A successful redirect back to checkout does not confirm settlement by itself. The platform must receive the provider notification or verify the payment through the configured integration.

## Separate account settings

Credentials and setup instructions for standard PayPal checkout are not interchangeable with the Friends & Family flow. Follow the instructions for the exact method you enabled.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  If a transaction exists in PayPal but an order is unpaid, verify the receiving account and notification configuration. Include the order and provider transaction references when contacting support; never share API secrets.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
