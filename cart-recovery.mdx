## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your plan">
    Abandoned cart emails are part of Premium. On Free, the switch stays off with an upgrade link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Open Email → Settings and switch on Send checkout reminders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the sender details">
    Reminders use the same From name and reply-to as your campaigns, so set those first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the results">
    After the first reminders go out, the same card shows how many were sent in the last 30 days, how many buyers came back, and the revenue they brought.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who gets a reminder

A buyer who entered their email at checkout and left the order unpaid gets one reminder about an hour later. Orders older than 24 hours are not reminded.

## Who is skipped

No reminder is sent if the buyer paid for another order since, unsubscribed from your emails, bounced, or already got a reminder from your store in the last 7 days. Orders created through the API with custom line items are skipped because there is no product page to link back to.

## What the email says

The reminder lists what they picked with the total, says nothing was charged, and links to the product in your store. It has an unsubscribe link; buyers who use it also stop getting your campaigns.

## How recoveries are counted

A reminder counts as recovered when the same buyer pays for an order in your store within 7 days of it being sent.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  If nothing is sent, check that your plan is Premium, the switch is on, the buyer entered an email, and they haven’t unsubscribed or been reminded this week.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
