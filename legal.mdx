## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Legal">
    Go to Storefront → Legal for the selected store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write store-specific policies">
    Explain your products, delivery process, support contact, refund conditions, and how customer information is used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and inspect the pages">
    Open the published terms and privacy pages from the storefront, not only the dashboard editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the checkout links">
    Review the policy links presented during checkout and make sure they point to your store’s actual policies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your store’s policies

Policies should describe your business and products. A platform’s own terms do not automatically explain your delivery promises or customer support arrangements.

## Custom themes

A custom footer or checkout integration can omit links that a default theme includes. After editing a theme, verify that visitors can still reach the relevant policy pages before making a purchase.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  If a policy link points to the platform’s legal pages instead of your storefront policy, check the hostname and the custom theme’s links. Relative links should stay on the store’s domain.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
