## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Customers">
    Choose the correct store and open Core → Customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the account">
    Search by the customer’s email or identifying details and check the purchase history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review balances carefully">
    A customer balance belongs to a particular store. It is different from your cryptocurrency wallet balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automate only when needed">
    Use the Customer Balance API with a key carrying customer_balance_api permission for supported lookups and balance changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Credit and debit workflows

Record your own reference for each adjustment and inspect the API response. Network errors can leave the outcome uncertain, so check the transaction history before repeating a request.

## Keep identities consistent

Use the same buyer email when matching a storefront purchase to a customer. Do not assume that a display name uniquely identifies an account.

## Balance at checkout

A balance payment requires the relevant customer session and enough available balance. Enabling the method does not grant a balance to every visitor.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  If a balance appears missing, confirm the store, email, customer session, and adjustment history. Do not confuse a store customer balance with funds available for a wallet withdrawal.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
