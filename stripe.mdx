## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your Stripe account">
    Use a Stripe account that is ready to process the products you sell. Resolve account restrictions in Stripe before testing your store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Stripe method">
    Go to Payments → Payment setup and open Stripe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the requested credentials">
    Copy the relevant key from the matching Stripe environment. Do not mix test credentials with a live transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable and verify">
    Save, enable the method, and confirm that a supported product checkout offers Card. Check the resulting payment in Stripe and in the order record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Provider and store state

An enabled toggle does not override an invalid key, a restricted provider account, or a product’s payment restrictions. Diagnose the provider response alongside the platform’s order status.

## Test and live payments

Use the environment supported by your configured integration. The onboarding and embed documentation demos are demonstrations and do not validate your Stripe credentials or send a charge.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  For declined or failed payments, inspect the provider’s reason and whether the correct credentials and account mode are configured. Never ask a buyer to send their full card number or security code to support.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
