## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your stock">
    Prepare the keys or items in the format expected by the product editor. Keep unrelated notes and formatting out of the stock input.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add keys to the right product">
    Choose the product and, when applicable, its variant before loading stock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check available quantity">
    Verify the stock count and per-order limits. Do not list more finite items than you can fulfill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify a completed order">
    Check that a confirmed purchase received the expected quantity and that delivered stock is no longer offered as available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Treat stock as sensitive

Keys are the goods you are selling. Do not publish a stock file in a storefront theme, a screenshot, a public code example, or a support message.

## Restocking

Add new unused keys when stock gets low. Before importing a batch, remove duplicates and verify that the keys are valid for the product edition you are selling.

## Do not reuse a delivered key

A delivered item may already have been redeemed. Investigate a failed activation against the order and product before making a replacement.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  If stock is unexpectedly unavailable, check recent orders and the selected variant. A stock display is not a guarantee that an old saved cart can still purchase that quantity.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
