## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the store and period">
    Open Core → Analytics and select the date range you want to inspect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the overview">
    Compare revenue and order counts for the same range. Check the currency and whether you are looking at completed payments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect products">
    Use product performance alongside the order list to understand what was sold.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Investigate an unexpected number">
    Find the underlying orders and compare their timestamps and payment status before assuming a chart is wrong.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use comparable ranges

A partial day is not directly comparable to a complete month. Timezone and range boundaries affect which orders appear in a view.

## Revenue is not always profit

Payment provider fees, refunds, fulfillment costs, and other business expenses can differ from the amount recorded on an order. Use provider statements and your own accounting records when reconciling money.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  For a mismatch, note the date range, timezone, store, currency, and order references that explain the difference.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
