## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the store and period">
    Open **Dashboard** → **Analytics**, then select the store and date range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the overview">
    Review **Revenue**, **Orders**, **Customers**, and **Avg. order value** for the same range. Use **Visitors** and **Views** for storefront traffic. **Overview** shows a short summary and your setup checklist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect sales and traffic">
    Compare sales by product, payment method, or customer. Compare traffic by country, device, or browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Investigate an unexpected number">
    Find the underlying orders and compare their timestamps and payment status before assuming a chart is wrong.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the conversion rate

In **Overview**, **Conversion rate** is the percentage of tracked storefront visitors who placed a paid order in the selected period. Orders that Paysum cannot match to a tracked visit do not count toward this rate. A dash means Paysum does not have enough data to calculate the rate.

## Track campaigns

Add UTM parameters to the storefront links you share. For example:

```text
https://your-store.mypaysum.io/?utm_source=discord&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=launch
```

Use the **UTM source** breakdown in **Analytics** to compare storefront traffic by source. The **Campaign** section on the order page shows the campaign for attributed orders.

## Connect external tools

Open **Integrations** → **App store** to set up Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, or Microsoft Clarity. This is separate from the metrics in **Analytics**.

## Use comparable ranges

A partial day is not directly comparable to a complete month. Timezone and range boundaries affect which orders appear in a view.

## Revenue is not always profit

Payment provider fees, refunds, fulfillment costs, and other business expenses can differ from the amount recorded on an order. Use provider statements and your own accounting records when reconciling money.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  For a mismatch, note the date range, timezone, store, currency, and order references that explain the difference.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
