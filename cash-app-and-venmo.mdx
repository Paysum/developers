## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure the method">
    Open Cash App or Venmo in Payments → Payment setup and add the receiving account details requested there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up receipt forwarding">
    Follow the method’s forwarding guide for the mailbox receiving payment receipts. Copy the current forwarding address from the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm forwarding works">
    Finish the email provider’s confirmation steps. Merely adding a destination may not enable forwarding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the whole flow">
    Make a controlled purchase, follow its payment instructions, and check that the receipt is matched to the correct order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Match the requested payment

The amount, recipient, and reference shown on the payment page help identify an order. An unrelated receipt or a screenshot is not the same as a confirmed matching payment.

## Forwarding accounts

Use the mailbox that actually receives receipts for the connected receiving account. Avoid rules that forward unrelated personal mail. Recheck the setup after changing an email account or payment handle.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  If payments are not matched, inspect the original receipt, forwarding status, receiving account, amount, and reference. Do not keep sending extra payments to force a match.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
