## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what you sell">
    Start onboarding and choose a product category. The choice personalizes the demo; you can change your catalog later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your store">
    Enter a name, an available store address, and a short description. Check the address before creating the store: it is used in shared links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add something to sell">
    Create a product with a title, price, and delivery method. For keys or files, add actual stock or a download before publishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect payments">
    Open Payments → Payment setup. Enable a method you have configured with the correct account credentials, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk through checkout">
    Open the storefront in a separate browser session. Check the product, price, email field, available payment methods, and delivery instructions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Try before you launch

Onboarding includes an interactive storefront and checkout demonstration. You can change quantities and complete a sample purchase there. The demo does not charge a card, create an order, or prove that your real payment account is connected.

## Finish at your own pace

Branding, your first product, and payment setup can be finished later. Your dashboard checklist keeps track of the remaining work. A store with no configured payment method cannot accept a normal paid order.

## Check a real payment

The demo and a live transaction serve different purposes. When you are ready, make a small real purchase using a payment method you control and verify both the order status and delivery. A real purchase may incur provider fees.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  If checkout has no payment options, check that a method is enabled for this store and has not been disabled on the product or variant.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
