## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your wallet">
    Open Payments → Wallet and make sure your store has a Litecoin wallet. Card payments are converted and paid out there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the method">
    In the dashboard, open Payments → Payment setup. Choose Card to crypto and select Turn on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on small orders">
    Card providers have minimum purchase amounts. Turn on auto raise to minimum if small orders should be rounded up to the provider minimum instead of hiding the method.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

The buyer pays by card through a partner such as Crypto.com or Guardarian, which buys crypto and sends it to your store wallet. The order is fulfilled once the crypto arrives.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  If the method doesn’t appear at checkout, check that the order meets the provider minimum or that auto raise is on. If a payment is slow, check your wallet history for the incoming transaction.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
