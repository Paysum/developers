## What to include

- Your store URL, the page where the problem occurred, and the time with its time zone.
- What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
- The order or checkout reference for a payment issue, or the endpoint, HTTP status, and request ID for an API issue.

<Note>Remove API keys, passwords, payment details, and customer information from logs and screenshots before you share them.</Note>
