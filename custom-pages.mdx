## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a page">
    Open Storefront → Custom pages and add a page with a clear title and a unique route.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the content">
    Add the information customers need. Keep headings descriptive and use links with meaningful labels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the page">
    Check the page within your chosen theme, including images, navigation, and the mobile layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it discoverable">
    Add a link to the page from your storefront navigation, footer, or another relevant page using your customization tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose routes carefully

A custom page should not replace essential routes such as checkout, cart, account, or existing legal pages. Prefer short addresses that describe the page, and update internal links when you change a route.

## Content and themes

The visual presentation depends on your active theme. Verify a custom page again after applying a different theme or changing the code that renders pages.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  If a link shows a 404, check the saved route, whether the page is published, and whether the link points to the correct store domain.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
