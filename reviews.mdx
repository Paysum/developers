## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read customer feedback">
    Open Feedback → Reviews. Use search and the visible columns to find a review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure a reward">
    Choose Create reward, select its requirements, and set the coupon settings shown in the dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage disputes">
    Open the action for a review that requires investigation and provide a specific dispute reason and description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track moderation">
    Use Feedback → Feedback to follow disputes and manage blocked emails or IP addresses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review rewards

A reward gives customers a reason to share feedback. Review its discount, maximum uses, prefix, and expiry before saving; the reward should make sense for your product margins.

## Moderation and evidence

A low rating alone does not explain a dispute. Provide the order context and the specific issue with a review so moderation can assess it.

## Search versus an empty store

An empty search result does not mean the store has no reviews. Clear the filter to return to the full list.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  If a review cannot be found, check the store, search filter, and purchase email. If a reward does not appear active, reopen its settings and confirm it was saved.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
