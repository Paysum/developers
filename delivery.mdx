## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the delivery type">
    Open the product editor and choose the delivery method that matches your product: keys, a download, buyer instructions, or a configured integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide the content">
    Add the actual keys, public file link, or message. A title and price alone do not supply anything to deliver.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test payment and delivery separately">
    Use the onboarding demo to explore checkout. To verify real fulfillment, use a live order and inspect what the buyer receives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the order">
    Check the completed order, the delivery information, and the receipt sent to the buyer’s email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment must be confirmed

Reaching a return page or receiving a browser event does not prove that a payment has settled. Delivery should follow the platform’s confirmed order state.

## Custom line items

A Checkout API session with custom line_items does not turn an arbitrary description into a configured product delivery. Use product_ids for products already in the store, or fulfill custom goods in your own backend after verifying the completed session.

## Support and missing delivery

Ask the buyer for the order reference and purchase email. Check payment status, available stock, the saved delivery configuration, and the delivery link before resending anything.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  An unpaid or confirming order is not a delivery failure. For a paid order, inspect stock, the product’s delivery settings, and whether the download can be accessed without signing into your personal account.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
