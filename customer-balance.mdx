## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the method">
    In the dashboard, open Payments → Payment setup. Choose Customer balance and select Turn on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add credit">
    Open Customers, choose a customer and add balance, or use the customer balance API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check out with balance">
    At checkout, signed-in customers with enough balance can pay with it. The order completes immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fees and refunds

Balance payments have no provider fee. Refunding to balance is a quick way to make good on an order without reversing a card payment.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  If customers don’t see the option, check that they are signed in with the email that holds the balance and that the balance covers the whole order.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
