---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Short answers to the questions sellers and developers ask most."
---

<AccordionGroup>
	<Accordion title="Do I need an API key?" defaultOpen>
		<p>
			Only for the API. Everything else - products, payments, delivery and design - is managed from the
			dashboard. When you do use the API, every request needs a key with the right
			<a href="https://docs.paysum.io/authentication">permission</a>.
		</p>
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title="Is there a rate limit?">
		<p>
			Yes. Creating checkout sessions and changing customer balances are limited to 100 requests per
			hour per key, and withdrawals to 50. See
			<a href="https://docs.paysum.io/api/rate-limits">Rate limits</a>.
		</p>
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title="Can I sell products I already created in my store through the API?">
		<p>
			Yes - create a checkout session with <code>product_ids</code>. Stock, variants and delivery work
			exactly as they do for regular store orders.
		</p>
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title="How do I know a payment succeeded?">
		<p>
			Subscribe with <code>webhook_url</code> and handle <code>checkout.session.completed</code>, or
			poll <a href="https://docs.paysum.io/api/checkout/get-session">Retrieve session</a>. Don’t rely on the
			customer reaching your success page.
		</p>
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title="Which cryptocurrencies can I withdraw?">
		<p>BTC, LTC and ETH, from the matching store wallet.</p>
	</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
