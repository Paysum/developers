## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a product">
    The customer chooses a product, variant, and quantity on the storefront or follows a hosted checkout link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide buyer details">
    Checkout collects the email address used for the receipt and delivery. A product can also require a Discord connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose payment">
    The buyer sees the payment methods allowed for this store and product, with any applicable fees or discounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and complete">
    The customer reviews the amount and policies, then continues through the selected payment method. Delivery follows confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Totals can change

Coupon discounts, gift cards, payment-method fees, and minimum adjustments can affect the final amount. Check the total shown after selecting the payment method, not just the product’s starting price.

## Return pages and order status

A browser can close or return before payment verification finishes. The order record and provider confirmation determine completion. Developers should use the retrieve-session endpoint or a verified webhook, not only a success URL.

## Embedded checkout

The embed uses the same store and payment configuration. Language selection changes the checkout labels without discarding the buyer’s entered details.

## If something does not work

<Note title="What to check">
  If a buyer cannot continue, check their email, required Discord connection, payment selection, stock, and policy acceptance. Inspect the displayed error before changing store settings.
</Note>

Still stuck? [Contact support](https://docs.paysum.io/support) with the store, the page, and any order or request reference.
